# Chapter 1: Arrival

The train reached the coast at dusk.[^2-fn1] Nobody met her at the station, which was, she decided, exactly as it should be.

She walked the length of the platform twice before choosing a direction.

> The sea does not care who arrives. — local saying

[^2-fn1]: The line had been threatened with closure since 1979.

# Chapter 2: The House

The house had three rooms and one working lamp. A list she found pinned to the door:

- Water on Tuesdays
- Post at the shop
- Do not feed the gulls[^3-fn1]
